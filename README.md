# Financial Comparison: Uber vs. Grubhub (FY 2019)

## Key Prompt
> "Use net loss attributable to Uber Technologies. Want two tables and a README with your key prompt and what you changed. The 2019 statements of operations in the Uber and Grubhub excerpts, read with Docling. Switch Grubhub's units to millions to align with Uber, for Uber have cost of revenue + operations and support becomes costs and expenses. Research and development and tech becomes tech. For loss lines keep total cost, net loss, and loss before income."

## Summary of Changes & Normalizations
1. **Unit Alignment:** Converted Grubhub’s financial line items from thousands (USD) to millions (USD) by dividing by 1,000 to align directly with Uber's reporting scale.
2. **Uber Expense Restructuring:** Combined Uber's *Cost of revenue, exclusive of depreciation and amortization* ($7,208M) and *Operations and support* ($2,302M) into a single line item labeled **Costs and expenses** ($9,510M).
3. **Tech Line Item Mapping:** Renamed Uber's *Research and development* ($4,836M) and Grubhub's *Technology (exclusive of amortization)* ($122.10M) to **Tech**.
4. **Loss Line Items Preservation:** Maintained strictly required loss metrics: **Total costs and expenses**, **Loss before income taxes** (Income (loss) before income taxes and loss from equity method investments for Uber; Loss before provision for income taxes for Grubhub), and **Net loss** (using Uber's Net loss attributable to Uber Technologies, Inc. of -$8,506M and Grubhub's Net loss attributable to common stockholders of -$18.55M).
5. **Percentage-of-Revenue Table Added:** Created a secondary analytical table breaking down each normalized expense category as a percentage of total revenue for a direct structural comparison.
